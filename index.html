AVERE introduces EmoReAlM, a benchmark for audiovisual emotion reasoning, and AVEm-DPO, a preference optimization framework that aligns multimodal large language models with emotion-centric audiovisual cues, reducing spurious correlations and hallucinated content.

Abstract (Preview)

Emotion understanding is essential for building socially intelligent agents. Although recent multimodal large language models show promising results, they often rely on spurious audiovisual cues or text-driven hallucinations. AVERE tackles these challenges by:

  • EmoReAlM: a benchmark evaluating cue–emotion associations, hallucinations, and modality agreement;
  • AVEm-DPO: a preference optimization method aligning model responses with relevant audiovisual evidence and emotion-focused prompts.
